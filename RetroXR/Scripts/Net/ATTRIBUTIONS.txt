Networking code — attribution
=============================

punchthrough.gd
---------------
  Ported from the netfox.noray addon, which implements the client half of the
  noray NAT punchthrough protocol.

  Source   : https://github.com/foxssake/netfox (addons/netfox.noray)
  Licence  : MIT
  Server   : https://github.com/foxssake/noray

  Ported rather than installed, for three reasons:

    - The addon reaches netfox core for NetfoxLogger and for a block of
      project settings. Taking it whole means taking the core addon, whose
      NetworkTime and NetworkRollback autoloads overlap with what
      netplay_session.gd already does.
    - It ships as two autoloads, Noray and PacketHandshake. This project drives
      the punch from NetworkManager at the moment a player asks for it, and a
      process-wide singleton is the wrong lifetime for something that exists
      only during a connection attempt.
    - The protocol is small: newline-separated TCP commands, a UDP registration
      and a status packet. Carrying a fork of an addon is more work than
      carrying this.

  MIT permits the reuse and does not affect the licence of this project.
