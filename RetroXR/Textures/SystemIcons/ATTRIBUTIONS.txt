Systematic icon set — attribution
=================================

Every .svg in this directory is from the "Systematic" theme by BAXY Square
(github.com/baxysquare), created for the Libretro project and shipped with
RetroArch and Lakka.

Files were renamed from "Manufacturer - System.svg" to "<systemid>.svg" to match
this project's systemids. Contents are unmodified: every row of the map below
was checked by git blob SHA against the commit above. Fourteen of our files are
byte-identical to more than one upstream name (several systems share one piece
of art — the MAME-derived ones especially); those rows name the file the copy
was taken from.

Each system also has a "-content" variant: the media you put in the machine
rather than the machine itself — cartridge, disc, tape, card. Those are here as
"<systemid>-content.svg" and are what the Cartridges browser draws. Two systems
have no content art upstream and fall back to their console: piece and ti_83.

Where art exists in several regional variants the North American one was taken,
verified by opening the file rather than trusting the suffix:

  super_nes      console: "SNS-001", the boxy North American machine, not
                 "SNSP-001" (the rounded cream PAL one, which is what this
                 directory shipped until 2026-07-30).
                 content: "-content (2) (U)". Both "(U)" files draw the US
                 SNS-006 cartridge body; (2) also has the top-edge notches and
                 the wide lower recess, where (1) has only a thin grip pill.
  dreamcast      content: the untagged file, not "(U)". "(U)" carries no swirl
                 at all, only greys; the untagged one has the orange #d05627
                 swirl worn by NTSC discs, against #4b5da7 blue for "(E)".
  master_system  content: the untagged file — the black SMS cartridge. "(J)" is
                 the white Mark III card.
  mega_drive     content: "(U)" — red, against blue for "(E)".
  nes            content: the untagged file — the grey 72-pin NES cartridge.
                 "(J)" is the red-and-white Famicom cartridge.
  pc_engine      content: "(U)" — orange, against blue for "(E)" and red for
                 untagged.
  playstation2   content: the untagged file — the silver DVD underside. "CD" is
                 the same disc with a blue #4b5da7 underside.
  sega_cd        console: "MK-1601-4102 (U)", the North American Model 1 drawn
                 with the Genesis it bolts to, framed landscape like the rest of
                 the set. NOT the untagged file, which is the bare CD unit seen
                 top-down and reads as an anonymous disc drive at tile size.
                 "MK-1631-4102 (U)" is the Model 2, equally North American; the
                 Model 1 is picked because its front-loading tray is the motion
                 MediaDimensions.FRONT_TRAY_SYSTEMS gives this system.

Retrieved 2026-07-28; -content art and the SNES console correction 2026-07-30.
