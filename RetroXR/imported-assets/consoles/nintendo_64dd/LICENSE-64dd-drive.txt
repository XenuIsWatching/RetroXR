MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

Adapted for RetroXR as n64dd_drive.glb by Tools/glb/prepare_64dd.py:

  * Converted from a Unity AssetBundle to glTF with ugc-to-gltf
    (https://github.com/XenuIsWatching/ugc-to-gltf).
  * The two bundles are the same mesh with different colour bakes, so one GLB
    is shipped with the retail colour map bound (n64dd_drive_color.png) and
    the dev kit's beside it (n64dd_drive_color_dev.png) for a runtime swap.
  * REMOVED the RCA cable bundle and its three plugs, the three finger-gesture
    empties, the two highlight empties and the three socket animations.
    RetroXR animates the disk and the leads itself.
  * Yawed 180 degrees so the slot faces +Z.
  * Nodes renamed: 64DD -> Shell, default (1) -> PowerLed, default -> LedLens
    and EjectButton, System Socket -> SocketMarker (moved to its seated pose).
  * The occlusion / roughness / metallic map was rebuilt from the bundle's own
    ao, roughness and metallic textures. The LED's emission is zeroed and
    switched on by the room with the console's power.
  * Colour, normal and ORM maps resized from 4096 to 2048 and written as PNGs
    beside the GLB.
