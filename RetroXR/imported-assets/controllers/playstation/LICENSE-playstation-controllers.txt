MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

Adapted by Tools/glb/prepare_ps1_pad.py:

  * Cleared the "SONY" and "PlayStation" wordmarks and the PS logo glyph from
    the base-colour sheet, filling with the pad's own sampled plastic grey.
    SELECT, START and the four face-button symbols are untouched — they are the
    only labelling the controls have. The normal map is flat across that FRONT
    region (only the button recesses are embossed), so no relief ghost remains
    there.

  * Blanked the compliance plate on the UNDERSIDE — "SONY", "CONTROLLER" and its
    fine print — which is embossed in the NORMAL map and carries no base colour,
    so the scrub above never touched it and the wordmark shipped, white on white,
    visible whenever the pad was turned over. The plate's moulded border and the
    screw bosses are left standing; only the lettering inside is flattened.
  * Rescaled to real-world size (145 mm across, a real SCPH-1080), re-origined
    centred on its own footprint and resting on y = 0.
  * Split the single mesh into its 105 disconnected islands and regrouped them
    into named controls — DPad, BtnCross, BtnCircle, BtnSquare, BtnTriangle,
    BtnSelect, BtnStart, BtnL1, BtnL2, BtnR1, BtnR2 — plus one PadShell. The
    geometry is unchanged; only the grouping is. This is what lets each control
    depress on its own.

Geometry is otherwise unmodified: 2,189 triangles, as published.

ps1_dualshock.glb — the DualShock
============================================================

Taken from "Sony Playstation" by Rusty Hardy (@rustificus), CC-BY-4.0 — the same
scene the console shell comes from. See
../../consoles/playstation/LICENSE-playstation.txt for the full credit.

MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

Adapted by Tools/glb/split_playstation.py:

  * Cleared the "SONY" and "PlayStation" wordmarks and the PS logo glyph from the
    Controler_Decals sheet. ANALOG, SELECT and START and the four face-button
    symbols are untouched: the marks start at y=619 and those legends end at
    y=617, so one rect takes all three and touches no labelling.
  * Rescaled to real-world size (145 x 60 x 92 mm, a real SCPH-1200), re-origined
    centred on its own footprint and resting on y = 0.
  * Reduced from 147,072 triangles to 9,285, welded first and with the imported
    custom split normals dropped and shading re-derived by angle.
  * Separated the D-pad into its own named mesh so the rocker can travel. The
    four face buttons, both shoulder pairs and SELECT/START are one connected
    surface in the artist's mesh and could not be separated.

The two plug ends in that scene are the same mesh rotated, so the one plug below
serves this pad as well.
