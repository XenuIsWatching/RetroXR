Shared scene assets — attribution and licences.

Assets here are used by MORE THAN ONE room, so they do not live under any one
room's folder. Scenes/Objects/furniture/table.tscn is the first of them: the
same table is spawned in the bedroom, the den and the arcade, and pointing it at
imported-assets/bedroom/ would have made the other two rooms load bedroom
assets to draw a table.

materials/Wood066_*
    "Wood 066" — ambientCG
    https://ambientcg.com/a/Wood066
    Smooth brown wood. Skins the spawnable table — top, aprons and legs, the
    last two a shade darker off the same set.

    Color, NormalGL and Roughness only, the same three the room materials keep:
    the pack also ships NormalDX (the wrong convention for Godot, so every bump
    would read as a dent) and Displacement (nothing here uses parallax, and it
    is expensive on the mobile backend).

    Sampled in OBJECT space by Shaders/pbr_surface.gdshader, because the table
    is a RigidBody the player carries around — in world space the grain would
    swim across the top as it moved.

materials/Cardboard004_*
    "Cardboard 004" — ambientCG
    https://ambientcg.com/a/Cardboard004
    Corrugated brown cardboard. Skins the spawnable storage box — outer walls,
    base and the four folded-out flaps, with a darker second material on the
    inner faces.

    Color, NormalGL and Roughness only, for the reasons the Wood066 entry gives:
    the pack also ships NormalDX (inverted green, so every flute would read as a
    groove) and Displacement (nothing here uses parallax). This set ships no
    AmbientOcclusion at all.

    Sampled in OBJECT space by Shaders/pbr_surface.gdshader — the box is a
    RigidBody the player carries, and in world space the corrugation would swim
    across it as it moved.
