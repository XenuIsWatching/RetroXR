Game Boy Advance cartridge (gba_cart.glb)

Own work. Photogrammetry reconstruction from Ryan McClelland's photographs
of a cartridge, rebuilt as clean geometry and decimated to 2,595 triangles
with a 1K baked atlas (base color, tangent normal, packed ORM). No label
artwork is included: the Label mesh carries a plain white placeholder that
the cartridge script covers with the scraped label art.

Licensed with the rest of RetroXR (see the repository LICENSE).

The front "Game Boy Advance" molding and the rear "Nintendo" wordmark are
left off the atlas; the model number and country/patent markings remain.
